### Planning Notes

* **Carbohydrate Quality & Glycaemic Load:** White polished grains are replaced with unpolished whole grains such as foxtail millet (thinai), barnyard millet (kuthiraivali), finger millet (ragi), samba broken wheat, and red/brown rice. These provide complex carbohydrates and soluble fibre, which digest slowly compared to refined grains.
* **Plate Proportion & Sequencing:** For lunch and dinner, aim to fill half your plate with non-starchy vegetables (poriyal, kootu, or greens), one-quarter with plant proteins (sambar, dal, chana, or curd), and the remaining one-quarter with complex carbohydrates. Eating fiber and protein first before your carbohydrates can assist in blunting rapid post-meal glucose spikes.
* **Smart Prep for 30-Minute Cooking:**
* Batch-cook dry legumes (chana, toor dal, whole moong) in a pressure cooker on Sunday and Wednesday to keep on hand in the refrigerator.
* Keep traditional spice powders (sambar podi, rasam podi) and peeled garlic ready to make seasoning fast without needing store-bought pastes.


* **Hydration & Lifestyle:** Pair these meals with consistent daily water intake (2.5 to 3 litres). A light 10- to 15-minute walk following larger meals (lunch and dinner) is an accessible, practical habit that works alongside dietary modifications to support muscle glucose uptake.
